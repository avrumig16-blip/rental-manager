ManageRent4U V110 — primary complete-board mapping

This is a mapping of implemented areas, not a pass certificate for every pictured
state. Service-backed actions retain the existing permissions and review flows.

01 Login → workspace: Auth88 Transition / Chooser; one/multiple workspace routing,
   loading and retry states retained. Chooser visually checked; live session retry
   and provider authentication not exercised.
02 Login and accounts: Auth88 sign-in, verification, passkey, recovery, reset,
   workspace switching and sign-out retained. Sign-in surfaces polished and checked.
03 Owner directory/workspace: directory list/cards, four metrics, portfolio/activity,
   linked properties/units/tenants, edit, bank details, archive and read-only retained.
04 Owner statements/rent updates/portal: financial collection, owner net statement,
   account details, preview/send review, schedules and owner confirmation retained.
05 Rent record/match/split/correct: shared Receipt95 allocation and review, exact
   cent suggestions, all earlier arrears, bank match and corrections retained.
06 Rent monthly workspace: filters/export, ledger/account drawer, statements,
   payments/charges/activity, balances and read-only retained.
07 Record sharing: Users & sharing → Shared links → choose/review; external scoped
   browse and revocation retained. No real share email sent during verification.
08 Team access: person + role → selected-property permissions → review; invitations,
   acceptance and access management retained. Selection/review checked locally.
09 Tenant directory/setup/profile: A–Z and total arrears, four-step inherited-address
   creation, ID import, four metrics, three information cards, activity/tasks and edit.
10 Owner messages/tasks/documents: existing scoped conversations, review before
   send, private/shared tasks and real files retained; owner tabs checked locally.
11 Rent statements/reminders/automation: period selection and original statement
   model, review before send, reminders/thanks, schedules and delivery states retained.
12 Tenant lifecycle: compact lease workspace and real contract generation/signing,
   provider handover/meter records, notices/indexation, access and closure retained.
13 Tenant messages/tasks/documents: conversations and recipient activity, task
   drawers, file visibility and original-file inline preview/retry checked locally.
14 Tenant payments/statements/reminders: ledger/account drawer, receipt/allocation,
   historical months/charges, statement PDF and payment communication retained.

Local fixture data varies from board examples. Real files, amounts, roles and
states come from the connected records. No example contract or balance substitutes
for a production record. See QA-V110.txt for actual test scope and live limitations.
