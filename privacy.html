ManageRent4U Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This policy explains how ManageRent4U processes personal data when you use our property-management platform, including the optional bank-account connection feature.

1. Who we are

ManageRent4U provides property-management software for managing owners, buildings, units, tenants, payments, expenses and related records. For privacy or data-protection questions, contact contact@managerent4u.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process account and workspace information, contact details, property and tenancy records, payment and expense records, documents you provide, support communications, technical/security information, and bank-account information that you expressly authorise us to access.

3. Open Banking and Enable Banking

When you choose to connect a bank account, ManageRent4U uses Enable Banking to facilitate access to account information made available by your bank. Enable Banking acts as an Account Information Service Provider when its authorisation is used. You are redirected through a secure authorisation and Strong Customer Authentication flow provided by the relevant bank/open-banking service.

ManageRent4U does not ask for or store your online-banking password or bank authentication credentials. You choose whether to authorise access and which eligible accounts are made available through the bank authorisation flow.

4. Bank information we may receive

Subject to your consent and what your bank supplies, the integration may provide account identifiers (including IBAN), account holder/name information, currency, balances, account details and transaction information such as dates, amounts, descriptions, references and counterpart information.

5. How bank information is used

Bank information is used to provide ManageRent4U features such as displaying connected accounts and balances, importing and reconciling rental payments, identifying outgoing transactions and expenses, and helping users organise financial records. ManageRent4U's current bank integration is intended for account-information access and does not give ManageRent4U your bank-login credentials.

6. Consent and disconnecting

Bank access is initiated only when an authorised user chooses to connect an account. You may remove a connected account in ManageRent4U. Your bank or Enable Banking may also provide mechanisms to review or terminate active data-sharing consent. Ending a connection stops future retrieval through that consent, subject to technical completion and any legal retention obligations.

7. Workspace isolation and access

ManageRent4U separates customer workspaces and applies role- and permission-based access. Banking information is intended to be available only to users who have been granted the relevant access within the applicable workspace. We use database and application controls designed to prevent one workspace from accessing another workspace's records.

8. Legal bases and purposes

We process personal data as necessary to provide requested services and perform our agreement with users/customers, to comply with applicable legal obligations, on the basis of consent where required, and for legitimate interests such as securing, maintaining and improving the service where those interests are not overridden by data-protection rights.

9. Service providers and recipients

We may use service providers necessary to operate ManageRent4U, including hosting/database infrastructure and, for the bank-connection feature, Enable Banking. Banks and open-banking participants process information under their own applicable terms and privacy notices. We do not sell personal data.

10. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including authentication, access controls and workspace-level restrictions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users must also protect their account credentials and devices.

11. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain required business and financial records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal obligations. Retention periods can differ by record type. Removing a bank connection does not necessarily delete historical financial records that were already imported and must legitimately be retained.

12. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the GDPR where applicable, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data, object to certain processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to a competent supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

13. Third-party services

The Enable Banking authorisation service and your bank may present their own terms and privacy information during the connection process. Their processing is governed by the notices applicable to their services.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as ManageRent4U changes or legal requirements evolve. The current version will be published at this URL with its latest update date.

15. Contact

Privacy and data-protection enquiries: contact@managerent4u.com
