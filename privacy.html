Property Manager Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Property Manager Pro processes information when the application is used for property management and read-only bank account connectivity.

1. Information processed

Property Manager Pro may process property-management information, tenant and contract information, and bank account information made available after an authorized user connects a supported bank account.

2. Bank account connectivity

The bank integration is intended for read-only account-information functionality. It may retrieve information such as account identifiers, balances and transaction information for reconciliation and property-management purposes. Property Manager Pro does not use this read-only connection to initiate bank transfers or payments.

3. Purpose of processing

Information is processed to operate the property-management service, display connected account information, reconcile incoming rental payments, maintain records, and provide related application functionality.

4. Access and security

Access to information is limited to authorized use of the application. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

5. Third-party services

The application may rely on service providers for hosting, database services and regulated/open-banking connectivity. Information may be transmitted to or processed by those providers where necessary to provide the requested functionality.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal, accounting and operational requirements.

7. Your rights

Where applicable, individuals may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction concerning their personal data, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements.

8. Contact

Questions or requests concerning privacy or personal data should be sent to the data-protection contact supplied for Property Manager Pro.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the application or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
