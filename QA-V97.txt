horizontal overflow in the checked states. 45 additional responsive checks completed

FOUR ISSUES FOUND AND CORRECTED
1. Duplicate step numbers in tenant setup and end-tenancy forms.
2. Allocation-month labels could clip the year; rows now give those fields more space.
3. Generic input styling covered the six verification-code boxes; specialised styling
   now preserves the visible boxes. Synthetic code entry survived a blur/focus cycle.
4. The lease-start date could wrap awkwardly on a phone summary card; date formatting
   is now kept readable in that card.

LIMITS
This is a local visual audit with synthetic/sample records and mocked backend responses.
It is not a pixel-by-pixel certification of every possible data combination or a new
live-provider test. Real email delivery, production access, bank connection, storage upload,
and signing-provider behavior require the deployed environment. Browser rendering checks
and visual consistency do not by themselves prove every end-to-end business operation.
